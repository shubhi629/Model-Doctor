## Reading these results

- *Reliability mode* `confidence_fallback` means the calibration split had fewer than 30 wrong (or right) predictions, so Model 2 could not be trained and ModelDoctor falls back to Model 1's calibrated confidence (identical to the baseline by construction).
- Model 1 is calibrated inside the train split, so on clean i.i.d. data its confidence is already a strong error detector and ModelDoctor often only matches it. Gains are expected where Model 1 is poorly calibrated (e.g. Decision Tree, KNN) or where input-quality signals carry extra information.
